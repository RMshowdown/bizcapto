Good questions

Do I need the internet?

No. Scanning, reading and saving all happen on the phone. Only the LinkedIn button opens your browser, and only when you tap it.

Which languages can it read?

It reads business cards (name cards) in the app's languages. English reads best. Other languages are still improving, so some words may come out wrong. That is why you review every card before saving.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on "App lock" in Settings, and BizCapto opens only with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. It uses your phone's own lock, so the app keeps no password of its own.

Where can I download it?

On Google Play, for Android 7.0 or newer. Save 10 cards first, then unlock unlimited cards with one purchase if you like it.

Is there an iPhone version?

It is planned. Android comes first and the iPhone version is still being built.

Which languages does the app use?

The app follows your phone's language. On Android 13 or newer, you can also pick its language in your phone's Settings. More languages are planned.

What happens if I change phones?

Export your cards to Excel on the old phone and import the file on the new one. There is no cloud copy, so keep that file safe.
